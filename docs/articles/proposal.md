# Final Project Proposal

## CDC data on waterborne illnesses

How does maternal cigarette smoking relate to infant birthweight? To
analyze this we will use the CDC data on USA births in 2025 and the
population will include all live births in 2025. Exposure variables
include maternal smoking during pregnancy through the recorded cigarette
counts. Unknown or missing responses will be excluded from the analysis.
The outcome is infant birth weight in grams. Some confounders include
maternal age, race, education (a common proxy for socioeconomic status),
pre-existing hypertenstion and pre-existing diabetes.

## NBA Stats

How well does an NBA player’s individual performance and their team’s
record predict whether or not they will be selected as an All-Star. The
dataset will include player and team record data from the 1979-1980 NBA
season through the 2025-2026 season. Exposure variables include player
individual per-game statistics such as points, 3-point shots made,
field-goal percentage, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, personal fouls
and turnovers as well as their team’s total wins. These will be
calculated as averages over all games played by each player, so there
should be no missing values once data is cleaned. The outcome variable
will be All-Star Selection at the end of the year. This is a binary
outcome variable so we will likely use logistic regression for the final
model. The team’s win record, the previous year’s win record and team
fanbase size may be confounders for this dataset, since all-star
selection has an element of popularity and familiarity.
